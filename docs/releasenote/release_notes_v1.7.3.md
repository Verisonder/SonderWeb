## SonderWeb 1.7.3

**Floating video window**
- Switch tabs while a video plays and it keeps playing in a small window over SonderWeb. Drag it anywhere, tap it to go back to its tab, or × to stop it.
- While a video plays, a new address, the home button and links to other sites open in a new tab, so the video keeps going.

**Picture-in-picture**
- YouTube can no longer stop a video in the small window or while it plays in the background. The play/pause button, the media controls and × still work.
- The temporary messages saying what paused a video are still on for now.

**Search**
- A typed address shows the site's own icon.
