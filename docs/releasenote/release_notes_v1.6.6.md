## SonderWeb 1.6.6

**Picture-in-picture**
- The video keeps playing in the small window instead of stopping after a second.
- Closing the small window with ✕ stops the video.
- Coming back to the app from the small window always brings back the bar, back and the page as it was.
- Opens on phones that don't do it by themselves (HyperOS), and knows a video is playing in more cases.
- New ⋮ menu item while a video plays: Picture-in-picture, to open the small window right away.

**Fixes**
- Fullscreen videos no longer get stuck at the top of the screen (YouTube).
