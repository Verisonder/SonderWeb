## SonderWeb 2.1.0

**Search widget apps**
- Drag an app by its handle to change the order.
- Each app sits on the left (after the logo) or the right (before the microphone).
- One icon size for all apps, and an app can have its own.
- A GIF can be an app's icon: it plays on the widget.
- The same app can't be added twice.

**Back gesture**
- The page behind shows far more reliably while you swipe back: a fresh picture is taken as you leave a page, the tab you're on never loses its pictures to other tabs, and while the next page is still loading it shows the page you just left.
