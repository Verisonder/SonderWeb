## SonderWeb 1.2.0

**Start page**
- Your own background: the theme's colour, one of 12 colours, or a picture from your phone as wallpaper, with a Darken slider so the text stays readable (Settings › Start page › Background).
- Shortcut icons look like apps on a home screen: sharp, without a grey tile behind them. Sites without an icon get their letter on a tile in a colour of their own.
- A (+) button at the bottom centre opens search.
- Choose how high the time and date sit (Settings › Start page › Time and date).

**Tabs**
- Each tab shows the site's small icon before its title.
- Tabs that come back when the app opens keep their title and picture, instead of a letter and their address.

**Browsing**
- The page fills the screen up to the very top once you scroll; the strip by the camera only shows at the top of a page.
- Going back from the first page of a site opens the start page; back on the start page leaves the app.
