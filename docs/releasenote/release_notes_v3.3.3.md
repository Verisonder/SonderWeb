## SonderWeb 3.3.3

**Widget icons**
- Animated WebP and animated PNG (APNG) work as app icons on the search widget, as well as GIF. Both keep soft, see-through edges, so they look as smooth as a still PNG.
- Animated icons play at their real timing: quick frames (like a blink) are no longer skipped, and a frame shown longer stays up as long as it should.
- GIF icons are sharper: their frames are kept bigger and shrunk smoothly, so their edges are less jagged. Pick a GIF again to get this.
