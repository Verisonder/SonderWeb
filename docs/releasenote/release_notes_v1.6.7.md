## SonderWeb 1.6.7

**Background playback**
- Sound and video keep playing when you leave the app, in picture-in-picture, and with the screen off.
- The phone's media controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen: title, picture, play/pause, next/previous where the site has them, and a seek bar.
- Private tabs show "Private tab" there, never the title or picture.
- Paused, the notification stays until you swipe it away; closing the tab or swiping SonderWeb out of recent apps stops the playing.

**Picture-in-picture**
- Videos no longer stop a second after going into the small window.
