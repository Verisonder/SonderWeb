## SonderWeb 1.1.2

**Address bar**
- Swipe the bar left or right to put it away, just like swiping it down: it stays hidden until you bring it back.
- To bring it back, tap the handle at the edge or slide it any way.
- The handle is a little bolder and follows the page: dark on light pages, light on dark ones, so it's always visible.
- With the bar on top, the handle sits at the top edge.
