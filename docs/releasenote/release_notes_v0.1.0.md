## SonderWeb 0.1.0

The first version.

- Firefox's engine with uBlock Origin built in. Tap the shield in the address bar to see what a page blocked.
- Search with !bangs: `!w`, `!yt`, `!gh` and more, before or after your words.
- Choose your search engine, or add your own.
- Tabs and private tabs, history, bookmarks and downloads.
- Strict tracking protection, HTTPS-only mode, and clearing browsing data in Settings.
- Can be set as the default browser.
