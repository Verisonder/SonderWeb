## SonderWeb 1.6.0

**Translate page**
- New in the ⋮ menu: translates the page into your phone's language, on the phone itself (nothing is sent to a translation service). The language pack downloads once, the first time.
- Show original puts the page back.

**Search**
- Tapping search opens a sheet on the keyboard: a big round field, voice search, and your latest sites with their icons.
- While typing: the search first, then !bangs, bookmarks and history, each with its site's icon.
- The page stays in view behind it; tap it or swipe back to close.

**Back gesture**
- Predictive back: the page follows your swipe with the previous page (or the start page) showing behind it. Let go to go back, swipe the other way to stay.
- On the start page the phone's own "back to home" animation plays.
- The search sheet shrinks and follows the swipe too.

**Tabs**
- Holding the tabs button no longer lists empty new tabs.
- Wide logos like Gmail's show whole in the tab lists.

**Site icons**
- Services that show a sign-in page when you're signed out (Drive, Docs, Calendar…) get their own icon instead of Google's "G".
