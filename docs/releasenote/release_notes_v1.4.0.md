## SonderWeb 1.4.0

**Start page**
- New default layout: the time sits a little higher, and the greeting, weather, search and shortcuts move up with it.
- No more jump when the weather loads: its space is kept, and the last reading shows straight away.

**Address bar**
- Hold the address, slide onto the "+" and let go for a new tab.
- Hold the tabs button (on sites and on the start page) for your tab list; slide onto one and let go to switch.
- The tab list shows the sites' own icons.
- Swiping the bar up puts it away, like swiping it down.
- While typing with the bar at the bottom, suggestions sit right above the bar.

**Sign-in**
- Email and password fields ask your password manager (Proton Pass, Bitwarden…) to show its suggestions.

**uBlock Origin**
- The shield panel has SonderWeb's look, and its sheet fits the panel.
