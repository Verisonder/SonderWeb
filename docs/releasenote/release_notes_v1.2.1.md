## SonderWeb 1.2.1

**Start page**
- Move the greeting, weather, search box and shortcuts together, higher or lower (Settings › Start page › Layout), next to the time and date setting.
- Sharper shortcut icons for big sites like YouTube, and small favicons show without a grey tile.

**Opening the app**
- "Start page" now opens the start page only when you open the app after closing it, not each time you come back to it. "After a while" does the same, and also when you come back after being away for the time you set.
