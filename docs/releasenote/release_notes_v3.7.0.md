## SonderWeb 3.7.0

**VPN (SonderWeb Sync)**
- Firefox's free built-in VPN, with your Mozilla account: Settings › Account and sync › VPN.
- Turn it on or off, pick a location (recommended or a country), and see how much of this month's data is left.
- A small key shows in the address bar while it's on (can be turned off).
- It covers SonderWeb's tabs, private ones too, not other apps. Mozilla decides the regions it's offered in and the monthly data.
- Already signed in? Tap "Set up the VPN" and sign in once more to give SonderWeb the VPN permission.

**Fixes**
- The tab count no longer includes empty new tabs, so it matches the tab list after you close tabs.
- Choosing a file on a page now works, including importing backups in uBlock Origin, AdGuard and AdNauseam.
