## SonderWeb 1.9.1

**Search widget**
- Fixed: the widget couldn't be added and the app closed when it tried to show it.
- New in Settings › Widget › Search widget: opens the widget's settings (applied to every search widget on your home screen), or adds one if there isn't any yet.

**Picture-in-picture**
- A video that stops in the small window starts again at once instead of after a second or two, and loading is restarted earlier so it usually doesn't stop at all.
