## SonderWeb 5.6.0

**New**
- Floating video window: pinch it with two fingers to make it bigger or smaller. Its size is kept for next time.
- YouTube: its bottom bar (Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, You) floats as a pill, like SonderWeb's own bar.
- SonderCircle: Updates in its settings. It shows when a newer version is out and installs it in one tap.

**Changes**
- Updates: SonderWeb keeps looking for newer versions after one is found (each time you come back to the app, at most every few hours), and "Check again" under a found update looks right away.
- Release notes in Settings › About show without Markdown marks.

**Fixes**
- Picture-in-picture: coming back to the app, YouTube's buttons showed the video as paused while it kept playing. They show playing again.
