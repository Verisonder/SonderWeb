## SonderWeb 4.5.1

**New**
- Inactive tabs close on their own: tabs not used for 14 days, and older copies of a page open in another tab, are closed after 3 more days. Change the number of days, or turn it off, in Settings › Tabs › Inactive tabs.

**Change**
- The tab count leaves inactive tabs out, so it shows the tabs you actually see in the tab list.
