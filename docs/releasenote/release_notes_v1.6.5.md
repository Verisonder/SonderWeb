## SonderWeb 1.6.5

**Picture-in-picture**
- Leave the app while a video plays and it keeps playing in a small window, shaped like the video, with a play/pause button. Tap it to come back.
- On by default; switch it in Settings › Tabs.

**Translate page**
- Choose the language to translate from and into. The last one you translated into is remembered.
- Pages that say they're in your language but aren't (x.com) can now be translated: just pick the real language.

**Shields**
- Settings › Privacy & security › Shields: a switch each for WebRTC IP protection, skipping AMP pages and skipping tracking redirects.
- The browser's own built-in parts no longer show in the Extensions list.

**Smoother**
- The search sheet rises out of the bar and sinks back into it; its rows come in one after another.
- Find in page slides in, the long-press menu slides away, the loading line glides, and the tab count rolls.

**Fixes**
- Turning desktop site off no longer leaves the page zoomed in and cut off (YouTube).
- The search sheet lists each site once.
