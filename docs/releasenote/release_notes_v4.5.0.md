## SonderWeb 4.5.0

**Fix**
- Address bar on top: pages no longer slide under the bar after you type an address. They start right below it.

**New**
- Tabs: swipe a tab left or right to close it.
- Settings › Tabs › Tab list: show tabs as a grid or a **list**, and keep a short list of tabs **at the bottom**, near your thumb.
- Settings › About: **Import bookmarks** and **Export bookmarks**, using the HTML file Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Brave and Safari use. Folders and order are kept; pages you already have aren't added twice.
- Settings › Start page › **Search field**: hide the search box under the clock (the + at the bottom still opens the search; one of the two always stays).
- Settings › Tabs › **Exit button**: adds Exit to the end of the ⋮ menu. It closes the app; your tabs are kept.
