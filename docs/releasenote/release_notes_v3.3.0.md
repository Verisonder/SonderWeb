## SonderWeb 3.3.0

**Firefox Sync, all of it (SonderWeb Sync)**
- Bookmarks, history and open tabs now sync both ways with Firefox, alongside passwords. Addresses and payment cards too.
- Choose what to sync has a switch for each: Bookmarks, History, Open tabs, Passwords, Addresses, Payment cards.
- Bookmarks come over with their folders, including Firefox's Bookmarks Toolbar, Bookmarks Menu and Other Bookmarks. Adding, renaming, moving and deleting carry over both ways.
- History you delete on another device goes here too, and the other way round.
- Your other devices' open tabs: a "From other devices" row at the top of the tab switcher. Tap a tab to open it here.
- Send to device: in the ⋮ menu, sends the page to another of your devices. Tabs sent to this phone open while SonderWeb is open.
- Addresses and payment cards: SonderWeb Sync offers to save the ones you type into forms and fills them in. Settings has the lists, to add, edit or delete. Card numbers are encrypted on the phone and only the last four digits are shown.
- Syncs on its own: when you open and leave the app, a few seconds after a change, and every 15 minutes while it's open.

**Both apps**
- Bookmark folders: make folders, rename, edit, move bookmarks between them, and delete (⋮ on each bookmark).
