## SonderWeb 3.5.0

**Copy everything to the other version**
- Settings › About › Copy everything to SonderWeb Sync (or to SonderWeb, from the Sync app). The other app opens and asks first.
- Copied: settings (look, search, privacy, shields, tabs, app icon), the start page (shortcuts, wallpaper, name, weather place), history, bookmarks with their folders, and open tabs.
- Settings are replaced; history, bookmarks and tabs are added to what's already there, so nothing is lost.
- Not copied: sign-ins to sites, extensions, site permissions, the Firefox Sync account with its passwords, addresses and cards, and home screen widgets.

**Search widget**
- The microphone and the picture button are now in the Apps list with your apps ("Apps and buttons"): drag them to choose the order, and put each on the left or the right.
- Widgets you already placed keep their look until you change them.
