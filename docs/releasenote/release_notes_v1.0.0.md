## SonderWeb 1.0.0

The first release: a private, minimal browser for Android on Firefox's engine.

**Privacy**
- uBlock Origin built in; add more extensions from addons.mozilla.org (⋮ › Extensions).
- Strict tracking protection, fingerprinting protection, bounce-tracking protection, trackers' cookies purged, tracking parameters stripped from links, HTTPS only, sites kept from probing your home network.
- Private tabs: pure black and kept apart from your normal browsing, locked behind your fingerprint, no screenshots or previews, incognito keyboard, Quad9 encrypted DNS.
- Optional encrypted DNS for every tab: Quad9, Mullvad or Cloudflare.
- Asks before opening another app: open it, or stay in the browser.

**The screen is the page**
- A floating bar that shrinks into a small button as you scroll; swipe the bar sideways to put it away, long-press the button to tuck it out of sight.
- Bar on top or bottom, floating or docked, any size; the button in any corner or anywhere you set.
- Hold the tabs button and slide up for a new tab; hold the address and slide onto a tab to switch; pull down to reload.

**Everything else**
- A start page with the time, a greeting, the weather and your shortcuts.
- !bangs and your choice of search engine; DuckDuckGo restyled to match the browser.
- History, bookmarks and downloads, all on your phone.
- Updates from inside the app: Settings › About.
