## SonderWeb 1.9.0

**Search widget**
- A new home screen widget: tap it to search, the mic for voice search, the frame to search with a picture (take a photo or choose one; searched on Google Lens).
- Its own settings when you add it: Dark, Light, Follow phone or Material adaptive theme; monochrome or coloured icons; show or hide the logo, mic, picture button and "Search" label; drop shadow; custom background, text and icon colours. A live preview shows the widget as it will look.

**Picture-in-picture**
- A video that stops loading in the small window is made to load again, instead of stopping after about 30 seconds.
- While a video plays in the small window or in the background, the phone is kept from freezing the page.
- Leaving right as YouTube pauses its own video still opens the small window.
- Coming back to the app no longer shows a black page: the video always goes back into its page.

**Floating video window**
- Throw it like picture-in-picture: it glides with your finger's speed and settles against the nearest side.
