## SonderWeb 1.5.3

**Shred site data**
- New in the ⋮ menu (replaces "Forget site when closed"): after you confirm, the page gets clawed apart, the site's tabs close and its cookies and site data are deleted. History and bookmarks stay.

**Coming back to the app**
- A page that comes back black or empty is redrawn by itself (and reloaded only if that doesn't help).
- No black flash while the page redraws.
- The ⋮ menu closes when you leave the app.

**Tabs**
- Holding the tabs button opens the tab list at the right edge, under your thumb, and its shadow is no longer cut off.

**History and bookmarks**
- Sites show their own icons.
