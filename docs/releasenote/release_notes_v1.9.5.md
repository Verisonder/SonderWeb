## SonderWeb 1.9.5

**Search widget**
- Looks closer to Google's search widget: a bolder mic, a camera-lens picture button, the text size and spacing, and the full width of the widget.
- New logo option: Arcs only (just the three arcs, in the icon colour).
- Tapping the logo opens the start page, without the search. The middle still opens the search.

**Start page**
- Holding an icon opens its menu (Edit, Remove) right away. Keep holding and drag to move it.
- New setting: Add button (the +). Always shown while you have no shortcuts.
- The last row is centred, and dragging lines up with it.

**Picture-in-picture**
- Leaving the app while the floating video window is showing (over Settings, the tab list, or from another tab) now opens picture-in-picture with that video.
