## SonderWeb 1.5.4

**Shred site data**
- New claw-mark icon in the ⋮ menu.
- After shredding you land on the start page.

**Coming back to the app**
- A page that comes back white or empty is now caught reliably and redrawn.
- Refresh also fixes a page that went blank while you were away.

**Tabs**
- Tab pictures are kept when you clear the cache, and stay right for sites that change address without reloading (Gmail, Skool).
- A fresh picture is taken when you leave the app.

**Loading**
- Logos like Gmail's and YouTube's show whole in the loading circle instead of being cut.
