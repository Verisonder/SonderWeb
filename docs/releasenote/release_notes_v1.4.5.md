## SonderWeb 1.4.5

**Privacy**
- WebRTC no longer reveals your local network addresses (calls still work).
- De-AMP: Google AMP links and AMP pages open the real site.
- Tracking redirect links (Google, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, LinkedIn and more) go straight to the real page.
- New in the ⋮ menu: "Forget site when closed" clears a site's cookies and data when its last tab closes (off unless you turn it on).

**Start page**
- Hold a shortcut and drag it to move it.
- Sharper site icons (YouTube and others), and Gmail shows its own icon.

**Browsing**
- A page that comes back empty after you leave the app reloads by itself.

**Updates**
- A downloaded update is kept: come back and tap Install, no second download. A download that was cut off carries on where it stopped.

**App icon**
- The icon is just the blue sphere, with no background.
- New squircle icon: switch in Settings › Appearance › App icon.
