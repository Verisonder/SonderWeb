## SonderWeb 5.0.0

**New: SonderCircle (circle to search)**
- A new small app in this release: `SonderCircle-5.0.0.apk`. Install it from here, or from SonderWeb's Settings › SonderCircle (which also updates it).
- Open it from its Quick Settings tile, its app icon, or as your phone's assistant (where the phone lets you choose one). It takes one switch under Accessibility, used only to take the screenshot when you open it.
- The screen glows and settles into a card. Draw around anything to search it by picture; tap a word to pick text (drag the handles for more), then Copy or Translate. Results slide up in a sheet, inside SonderWeb, with your own sign-ins.
- Two fingers zoom in and move the screenshot around.
- The translate button paints the translation over the text on the screen. Hold it to pick the language (your phone's comes first).
- SonderCircle's settings (⋮ on the circle screen): search text with SonderWeb's own engine or another (!bangs work), and pictures with Google Lens, Bing or Yandex.

**Change**
- Search widget: tapping the SonderWeb logo now just opens the app, on the page you were on (no new tab).
