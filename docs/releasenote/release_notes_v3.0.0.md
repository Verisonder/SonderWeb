## SonderWeb 3.0.0

**Two apps now**
- **SonderWeb**: the browser as before.
- **SonderWeb Sync** (new): the same browser with Firefox Sync. It's a separate app (`SonderWeb-Sync-3.0.0.apk`), so both can be installed side by side. Each updates to its own APK.

**Firefox Sync (SonderWeb Sync)**
- Sign in with your Mozilla account: the first launch asks, or tap Sign in at the top of Settings. The sign-in tab closes by itself once you're in.
- Your account at the top of Settings, with your picture, name and email. Account and sync: Manage account, Sync now, Device name, Sign out, and Choose what to sync.
- Passwords sync both ways with Firefox on your other devices. SonderWeb Sync offers to save the passwords you sign in with and fills them in. Settings has the list, to show, copy or delete them.
- Passwords are encrypted on the phone before they're synced: Mozilla can't read them.
- Only passwords for now. Bookmarks, history and open tabs come later.

**Both apps**
- The address bar is at the bottom by default (if you never moved it).
