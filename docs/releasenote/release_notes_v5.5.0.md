## SonderWeb 5.5.0

**New**
- Undo closed tabs: closing tabs (×, swipe or Close all) shows "Tab closed · Undo". Undo brings them back in their places, with their back and forward pages. (#9)
- Start page › Advanced customization (all off until you switch them on): rows of Collections (your own sets of sites), Bookmarks, Most visited, Recently visited and Recently closed tabs under the shortcuts. (#12)
- Settings › Tabs › Tab list: Layout switch (grid and list icons at the top of the tab list) and Close with × (instead of Done). Both off, so the tab list looks as before unless you turn them on. (#7)
- Settings › About › Import / Export: settings, start page and bookmarks to a file or from one. Choose which; history and open tabs are never included. Bookmarks alone are saved as an HTML bookmarks file other browsers can read, and bookmarks from other browsers can still be imported. (#8)

**Changes**
- Search suggestions: each matching bookmark is listed, with its address under its name. (#10)
- Settings: coming back from a page returns to the same place in the list. (#11)
- Messages now show over Settings and the tab list, so imports and exports say when they're done or failed. (#8)
- Default browser: says thank you once SonderWeb is your default.
- Lighter pages: with picture-in-picture off, the video helper no longer runs on pages; with all three shields off, neither does the shields helper.
