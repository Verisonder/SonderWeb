## SonderWeb 3.8.5

**Profiles**
- A profile's picture can be the picture of the Google account signed in on it (picked up when you visit a Google page). A picture you choose yourself comes first; Settings › Profiles › Google account pictures turns it off.
- Signing in to a second Google account in the same profile asks whether to give it its own profile (named after the account, with Google's sign-in ready there), or to switch to the profile that already has it. "Keep here" stops asking.
- Settings › Profiles shows each profile's Google account.
- Private tabs show a hat-and-glasses picture instead of the profile's.
- The profile picture's ring sits evenly in the bar.

**Fixes**
- The normal SonderWeb no longer offers to save passwords (only SonderWeb Sync saves them, with "Save and fill passwords" on).
