## SonderWeb 1.8.0

**Picture-in-picture**
- While a video is in the small window or playing in the background, YouTube can no longer pause it. Only your own controls can: the small window's play/pause, the floating window's buttons and the media notification.
- The temporary messages about what paused a video are gone.

**Menu**
- New tab, New private tab and Exit are no longer in the ⋮ menu.
